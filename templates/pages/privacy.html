PRIVACY POLICY

We respect student privacy and collect only the minimal data required to coordinate micro-tasks.

What we store: Your username, email address, bounty details, and claim messages.
Contact visibility: Your email and optional contact handle are never shown to anyone before a claim is accepted. After the poster accepts a claim, only those two people see each other's email and contact handle.
Campus verification and your ID card: We never store a photo or scan of your ID card. For verification we keep a one-way keyed hash of your registration number plus its last 4 digits, and how and when you were verified. A second account using the same number can still be verified, but it is not bound to the number and is flagged for a moderator to review. For campus-email verification we keep the address that was verified; for an in-person check we keep which volunteer checked you. If a volunteer checked your physical card, we also keep the name printed on it, encrypted, which apart from you only a moderator can view, and every moderator view is recorded with a reason. We cannot recover your full registration number from what we store.
Moderator access: Moderators can look up which account holds a registration number and can, with a recorded reason, view an account's contact details. Each of those actions is logged with who did it and why.
Cookies & Sessions: We use essential HTTP cookies for CSRF protection, session management, and your preferred board layout (grid or list). We do not use third-party tracking, advertising cookies, or analytics beacons.
