RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

CAMPUS CODE OF HONOR

1. NO ACADEMIC DISHONESTY:

Do not post requests for live exam assistance, proxy attendance, or graded quiz submissions. Homework debugging, tutoring, and proofreading are permitted.

2. HONOR THE REWARD:

When a peer completes work meeting the agreed bounty description, pay the promised reward via UPI or cash immediately upon completion.

3. DELIVER ON TIME:

If you claim a bounty and get accepted, prioritize it before the posted deadline. If you cannot finish, notify the poster promptly.

4. SAFE CAMPUS MEETUPS:

For offline handoffs (hardware, notes, campus errands), meet in well-lit public campus spaces (library, cafeteria, student activity center).
