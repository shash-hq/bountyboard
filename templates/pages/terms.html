Offline Settlements: Bounty Board does not hold, process, escrow, or guarantee funds. All financial settlements occur strictly offline between users.
Student Responsibility: Users are solely responsible for verifying the identity of other students, the quality of deliverable work, and honoring payments.
Private arrangements, not university work: Every task on Bounty Board is a private arrangement between the people involved, including tasks posted by faculty or staff. A task is never official university work, and nothing here is endorsed, supervised, or paid for by Lovely Professional University. A "Staff" badge only means the staff role is on record for that person (granted by a moderator or from a list of staff addresses) and they are currently campus-verified; it does not make the task an assignment.
Campus verification: Accepting a claim, or being accepted, requires a current campus verification, so that contact details are shared only between two checked people. Verification can be revoked, for example for a policy violation or an invalid check. If the other person's verification is revoked as invalid or for a policy violation, you may cancel the bounty without penalty. Leaving campus or an expired verification does not give that option.
Moderation: Campus moderators hold full discretion to remove, hide, or ban content and accounts that violate academic policies, spam, or harass students.
