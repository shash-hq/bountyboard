WHAT IS BOUNTY BOARD?

Bounty Board connects university students who need quick tasks solved with peers who have the skills and time. No middlemen. No transaction fees. No corporate fluff.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Post a Bounty: Describe the task, set a reward (₹10 to ₹10,000) and pick a strict deadline.
  2. Review Claims: Classmates send their contact handle and offer to help.
  3. Accept One: The poster picks exactly ONE claimant. Other pending claims get rejected automatically.
  4. Settle Offline: Connect via WhatsApp/email, finish the work in person or online, and pay via UPI or cash.
  5. Mark Done: The poster marks the bounty complete. Reputation built!
